Change notes are a vital tool for managing changes in operational processes, ensuring that the organization can adapt to changes without disrupting the established workflow. These notes are used to document changes, including the reasons for the change, the impact of the change, and the steps taken to implement the change. The review process for change notes involves a detailed examination of the content, ensuring that all changes are accurately recorded and that the impact of the change is clearly understood. This process is typically initiated by the change manager or the person responsible for implementing the change, who then seeks feedback from stakeholders to ensure that the information is accurate and relevant. The review process is iterative, involving multiple rounds of feedback and revisions until the change notes meet the required standards. This ensures that the change notes are not only accurate but also useful for future reference and analysis.
